# SafeSite – Support

**SafeSite** is a free browser extension for Safari, Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Firefox, made by **Athelstan Services Ltd**. It is provided "as it is", with no formal support service or guaranteed response.

## Supported browsers

- **Safari on iPhone and iPad:** iOS and iPadOS 17 or later
- **Safari on Mac:** macOS 26 or later
- **Chrome and Microsoft Edge:** desktop
- **Firefox:** desktop and Android

## Contact

[safesite@athelstanservices.co.uk](mailto:safesite@athelstanservices.co.uk). Include your device, its system version, your browser and your SafeSite version (SafeSite Settings → About).

## Common questions

**Turning SafeSite on in Safari**
- **iPhone and iPad:** Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions (on iOS 17: Settings → Safari → Extensions) → SafeSite. Turn on **Allow Extension** and set **All Websites** to **Allow**.
- **Mac:** Safari → Settings → Extensions → tick **SafeSite**, then **Edit Websites…** and allow it on all websites.

SafeSite needs access to all websites so it can check sign-in and payment forms before you type. Pages are checked only on your device.

**Why did I get a warning?**
The site asked for a password, card number or similar details, and it isn't on your list of safe sites. Check the site name in the warning. If it's the site you meant to visit, tap **Trust this site**. Open SafeSite from the toolbar (or Safari's page menu on iPhone) and tap **Explain** to see what was found.

**Marking sites safe or unsafe**
Open SafeSite for the page and choose **Mark as Safe** or **Mark as Unsafe**. Manage the full list in SafeSite's Settings.

**Turning warnings off for a site**
Open SafeSite for the page and turn checks off for this tab or for the whole site. Turn them back on in the same place.

**No notifications on iPhone or iPad**
Notifications are off by default there; SafeSite warns you at the top of the page instead. To turn them on: SafeSite Settings → **Show Notifications**, and allow notifications for SafeSite in the iPhone's Settings → Notifications. If they stop after an update, close Safari fully and open it again.

**Moving my safe sites to another device or browser**
SafeSite Settings → **Export**, then **Import** the file on the other device. Sites already on the list aren't added twice. On a Mac, **Sync with other browsers** keeps Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox in step automatically.

**The AI check**
It's optional and off by default; turn it on in SafeSite's Settings. It runs on your device and can add or confirm a warning, but never marks a site as safe. See the [privacy policy](privacy) for details per browser.
