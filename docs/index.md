# SafeSite

**Warns you before you type a password or card details on a site you haven't marked as safe.** For Safari on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and for Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Phishing sites copy real sign-in pages. SafeSite keeps your own list of sites you trust. When any other site asks for a password or payment details, it highlights the field and warns you, showing the real name of the site you're on.

## Features

- Your own list of safe sites: add the site you're on, type one in, or import bookmarks from Safari, Chrome, Firefox or Edge
- A clear warning when an untrusted site shows a password, card number or similar field, with **Trust this site**, **Leave** and **Not now**
- Pages on plain HTTP that ask for sensitive details are flagged
- A page icon and toolbar button showing whether the site you're on is safe, unsafe or unknown
- Mark any site safe or unsafe, or turn warnings off for one tab or site
- **Explain** shows why a page was flagged
- An optional AI check that runs entirely on your device; it can add or confirm a warning but never marks a site as safe
- Export your safe sites and import them on another device or browser; on a Mac, Safari, Chrome and Firefox can keep the same list

**Privacy:** SafeSite collects no data. It has no accounts, ads or tracking, and checks pages only on your device.

## Supported browsers

- **Safari on iPhone and iPad:** iOS and iPadOS 17 or later
- **Safari on Mac:** macOS 26 or later
- **Chrome and Microsoft Edge:** desktop, from the Chrome Web Store
- **Firefox:** desktop and Android
